Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's what we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order or create an account, we collect your name, email address, shipping address, and payment information. Payment details are processed securely through Shopify Payments — we never see or store your full card number.

We also collect basic browsing data (pages visited, time on site) through Shopify's built-in analytics and cookies. This helps us understand how people use the site so we can improve it.

How we use it

We use your information to process and fulfill your orders, send order confirmations and shipping updates, and respond to your questions. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties except as needed to fulfill your order — specifically our supplier network and Shopify's platform.

Email marketing

If you sign up for our email list, we'll send you updates about new products, promotions, and sustainability content. You can unsubscribe at any time — every email has an unsubscribe link.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to keep your shopping cart working and to understand site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work properly.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, email us at hello@smartbash.co.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
