Shipping Policy

We ship across the United States. International shipping is not currently available.

How we ship

SmartBash works with a curated network of sustainable suppliers across the US. Because we ship directly from the source — what we call eco-delivery — your order may arrive in multiple packages from different suppliers at different times. This means fewer warehouses, less handling, and a lower carbon footprint than traditional retail.

Delivery times

Shipping times vary by supplier and product. Estimated delivery times are shown at checkout and vary by supplier and destination.

If you've ordered multiple items, don't be surprised if they arrive separately — that's eco-delivery doing its thing.

Shipping costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your order and supplier. You'll see the exact cost before you complete your purchase.

Questions?

Reach us at hello@smartbash.co and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.
